KEY FINDINGS:

1. COMPRESSION RATIO
   OpenZL (trained) achieves 8.21x vs Snappy's 4.24x — a 1.94x
   improvement. For 118 MB of TLV data:
     - Snappy:  27.9 MB
     - OpenZL:  14.4 MB  (48% less than Snappy)

2. DECOMPRESSION SPEED
   OpenZL decompresses at 2,366 MB/s — within 19% of Snappy's
   2,928 MB/s. Both are memory-bandwidth bound at these speeds.
   For 59 KB chunks, decompression takes ~25 microseconds each.

3. COMPRESSION SPEED
   OpenZL compresses at 141 MB/s vs Snappy's 1,351 MB/s.
   Per 59 KB chunk: OpenZL ~0.4 ms, Snappy ~0.04 ms.
   This is the tradeoff for 2x better compression.

4. TRAINING VALUE
   Training improves ratio from 7.85x to 8.21x (+4.6%) while
   maintaining equivalent decompression speed (2,366 vs 2,304 MB/s).

INFRASTRUCTURE IMPACT (projected)
------------------------------------------------------------
Based on production numbers from the pipeline reference:

nom-dns-base topic:
  Current (Snappy):  ~150 GB on disk (8 partitions × 18.7 GB)
  With OpenZL:       ~77 GB on disk (48% reduction)
  Savings:           ~73 GB per POP

nom-dns-vertica topic:
  Current (Snappy):  ~33 GB on disk (20 partitions × 1.7 GB)
  With OpenZL:       ~17 GB on disk
  Savings:           ~16 GB per DC

Per-POP savings:     ~73 GB disk, proportional network reduction
Across N POPs:       73 × N GB total savings

Additional benefits:
  - Less WAN bandwidth for POP→DC mirroring
  - Longer Kafka retention with same disk budget
  - Less broker I/O for replication
  - Near-identical decompression latency (25 us per chunk)
